## 🎓 What We Learned (Meta)

By analyzing these 7 frameworks, we discovered:

1. **Lifecycle Hooks** are the foundation of observability (not just logging)
2. **Compression + Progressive Disclosure** beat full-context injection 10:1
3. **Failure capture** beats post-mortems (learns automatically)
4. **Template-based docs** beat hand-maintained (never stale)
5. **Sub-agent isolation** beats raw context (less hallucination)
6. **Deterministic workflows** beat "intelligent suggestions"
7. **Multi-platform compatibility** is non-negotiable

→ **Grimoire can uniquely combine all 7** in one coherent framework
